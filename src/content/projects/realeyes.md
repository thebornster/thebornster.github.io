## The question

We didn't want to use AI for the sake of it. We started from one question: how do you make the screen feel alive?

## What we built

An immersive game with dynamic dialogue, NPCs that react to you, and dread that is personal to each player. Everything runs through your webcam in real time. The game reads your face and your hesitation, and the characters respond to what they see.

## Result

1st place in the Supercell track and the Crowd Favorite prize. Built with Arda Karaköz, Kiara Basa and Dang Vu Hai Dang.
